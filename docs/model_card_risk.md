# Model card: txlens-rejection-risk v1

**Trained on synthetic data, not clinically validated, decision support only.**

Generated by `make risk-train` from data extracted 2026-09-30. Full evaluation: `evals/reports/risk_model.md` (`make risk-report`).

## Intended use
- Decision support at scheduled transplant surveillance visits: ranks visits by estimated 90-day risk of biopsy-proven rejection. Not a diagnosis.
- Users: transplant clinicians and data stewards reviewing the surveillance worklist. Researchers never see individual scores.
- The score is a prompt to look at the patient, next to the rule-engine flags. It never replaces a biopsy, and no action is taken automatically.

## Out of scope
- Real patients: the model has only ever seen synthetic data.
- For-cause (unscheduled) dd-cfDNA draws, pediatric recipients, organs other than kidney and heart.
- Diagnosing rejection or choosing a treatment.

## Model
- Champion: **Logistic regression** with {'C': 1.0}. Rule fixed before testing: gradient boosting wins only if its cross-validated AUPRC beats logistic regression by at least 0.01.
- Prediction: at a scheduled surveillance visit, once its results are back. Outcome: a biopsy showing TCMR, ABMR or mixed rejection in the next 90 days (borderline counts as no rejection).
- Production version: the champion refit on all three centers. Its performance is estimated by the internal and external tests below, which it did not take part in.

## Features (all known on or before the visit day)
| Feature | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| dd-cfDNA (log) | log % | Natural log of the visit's dd-cfDNA. |
| dd-cfDNA trend | % per month | Change since the previous scheduled dd-cfDNA, per month. |
| dd-cfDNA vs own lowest | ratio | Visit dd-cfDNA divided by the patient's lowest earlier scheduled value. |
| Creatinine change | % | Latest creatinine vs the median of days 30-90 after transplant. |
| Tacrolimus variability | CV % | Coefficient of variation of tacrolimus troughs in the last 180 days. |
| DSA positive | yes/no | Any donor-specific antibody result with MFI >= 1000 so far. |
| Time since transplant | months | Months since transplant. |
| Prior rejection | yes/no | A biopsy-proven rejection recorded before this visit. |
| Heart graft | yes/no | Heart (1) or kidney (0) transplant. |
| Deceased donor | yes/no | Deceased (1) or living (0) donor. |
| Delayed graft function | yes/no | Delayed graft function recorded on or before this visit. |

## Limits
- Synthetic data: the generator raises dd-cfDNA and creatinine 14-56 days before a rejection biopsy and makes DSA appear before ABMR. Tacrolimus variability, donor type, delayed graft function and age have no built-in effect, so the model cannot learn one. Real-world performance is unknown.
- Labels come from OMOP: pathology reports still in the review queue are missing and clinic-note mentions fill part of the gap. The evaluation compares them with the generator's truth.
- The draw reason never reaches OMOP: a visit is the first dd-cfDNA draw in a schedule window, so some for-cause draws are included (measured in the evaluation).
- Heart recipients are few; their results have wide intervals.

## Monitoring
- Every prediction stores the model name and version, its inputs and top contributions.
- Each nightly run stores the population stability index (PSI) of the scores against the training reference; the evaluation report adds feature PSI. PSI above 0.25 means the inputs have shifted and the model should be re-validated before further use.
